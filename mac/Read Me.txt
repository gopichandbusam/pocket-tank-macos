Pocket Tank for Mac — 1.3 alpha

Open Pocket Tank.app and complete the original welcome screens. The original trained fish model runs locally; no cloud account or internet connection is required.

Move the tank by its title bar. Resize it proportionally or close it with the standard red window button. Click ⓘ for scrollable, resizable, system-themed care instructions and Feed, Light, Milestones and Sound controls. Command-? also opens Instructions.

Float above other apps using the checkbox/menu. Transparency fades the aquarium down to 20% opacity; Instructions stays readable. Opaque restores its appearance. Window and transparency preferences persist.

Command-Q saves and quits. Normal close/quit also saves to:
~/Library/Application Support/Pocket Tank/tank.sav

Keep this data outside the app so an update preserves your aquarium. A failed save offers Retry Save / Keep Tank Open. Use one running copy: independent instances do not coordinate writes.

Care gestures:
- Click the water surface or drag downward near the top to feed.
- Click a fish for stats; MORE opens its milestones/settings/upgrades.
- Hold the mouse on the glass for a few seconds; trusting fish may approach.
- Drag across algae to clean; swipe sideways through plants to trim.
- Two quick clicks switch manual light; three can startle fish.
- Quiet fish may be resting or inspecting plants, especially at night.

The DMG includes Uninstall Pocket Tank.app. Review its removal list and confirm; it can optionally remove aquarium data and preferences. Recognized app/data copies are moved to Trash.

Current download: Apple Silicon, macOS 27+. Independently maintained alpha; ad-hoc signed, not Apple notarized. Older macOS and Intel downloads are not verified. Original firmware-style settings and developer shortcuts remain inherited.
